Clear 2D floor plans for property listings

A floor plan helps people understand the arrangement and flow of a property. FrameCrest turns suitable source information into a clean visual layout with readable room labels and dimensions where reliable measurements are provided.

What the service can include

  • Clean 2D floor plan presentation
  • Room names and labels
  • Dimensions taken from supplied or approved source information
  • Consistent formatting for property marketing
  • Preparation from sketches, measurements or approved plans

Measurement accuracy matters

We do not invent room sizes or guess missing measurements. If the source information is unclear or incomplete, we ask for clarification. The finished marketing graphic is not a substitute for a certified architectural, engineering, surveying or compliance document.

How the process works

Share your source files, reference style, output requirements, project volume and preferred deadline. We review the brief, confirm scope and pricing, complete the agreed work, carry out a quality check and deliver the files in the agreed format.

Who this service supports

FrameCrest works with real estate photographers, photography and media studios, agents, brokerages, property managers and property marketing teams. One-off projects, recurring batches and approved white-label arrangements can be discussed.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need to prepare a floor plan?

Provide measurements, a sketch or an approved source plan with enough detail to understand the layout.

Can you estimate missing dimensions?

No. Missing or unclear measurements must be confirmed by the client or source provider.

Are your floor plans certified architectural drawings?

No. The service is for clear marketing presentation based on client-supplied information.
