YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice.

This notice explains how FrameCrest Studios handles information submitted through the project enquiry form and how you can contact us about it.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Information you provide

When you send a project enquiry, we may receive your name, email address, company or studio name, the service you are interested in, estimated image or property volume, preferred project frequency, turnaround preference and the details you include in your message.

How we use it

We use enquiry information to understand your request, respond to you, discuss project scope and prepare a quote or next steps. We do not ask you to submit passwords, access codes, payment card details or other sensitive personal information through this form.

Form delivery provider

The website uses FormSubmit to receive form submissions and forward them to framecrest.media@gmail.com. This means information entered into the form is transmitted to FormSubmit as part of delivering your enquiry. FormSubmit's documentation states that it retains submissions for 30 days. Its own privacy terms explain how it handles information processed through its service.

Read FormSubmit's privacy terms for details about its service. The email copy delivered to our Gmail inbox may remain there until it is deleted.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or active project and maintain appropriate business records. Email copies are managed in our inbox. You can contact us to ask about an enquiry you submitted.

Your choices

You can choose not to use the enquiry form and email us directly instead. You may also contact us to ask us to correct or delete information associated with your enquiry, subject to any record-keeping requirements that apply.

Contact

For questions about this notice or information you have sent us, email framecrest.media@gmail.com.
