Property walkthrough and promotional video editing

Real estate video editing turns supplied footage into a structured presentation that helps viewers follow the property and notice its key spaces. The edit is guided by the intended platform, the footage available and the style agreed in the brief.

What video editing can include

  • Footage selection, trimming and sequencing
  • Property walkthrough editing
  • Colour correction and visual consistency
  • Transitions and considered pacing
  • Titles, captions and supplied branding elements
  • Exports in agreed formats and aspect ratios

Footage, music and usage rights

Clients supply footage and confirm they have the rights or permissions needed for its use. Music, fonts, logos and other third-party assets must be supplied with appropriate rights or agreed as licensed materials. Final delivery specifications are confirmed before editing begins.

How the process works

Share your source files, reference style, output requirements, project volume and preferred deadline. We review the brief, confirm scope and pricing, complete the agreed work, carry out a quality check and deliver the files in the agreed format.

Who this service supports

FrameCrest works with real estate photographers, photography and media studios, agents, brokerages, property managers and property marketing teams. One-off projects, recurring batches and approved white-label arrangements can be discussed.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide filming?

This service is for editing footage supplied by the client. Any filming requirement must be discussed separately.

Can you prepare videos for social media?

Yes. Share the intended platform, aspect ratio, duration and any branding requirements in the brief.

Can you add music and titles?

Titles and music can be included where agreed and where the necessary usage rights are in place.
