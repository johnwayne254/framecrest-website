Professional property photo editing

Real estate photo editing helps prepare listing photographs for websites, portals, brochures and social channels. FrameCrest works from client-supplied images and references to create a balanced, consistent finish while respecting the actual property.

What the service can include

  • Exposure, brightness and contrast correction
  • Colour and white-balance correction
  • HDR blending from suitable bracketed exposures
  • Window-view balancing where source files allow
  • Straightening verticals and correcting perspective
  • Consistent finishing across a complete property set
  • Exporting agreed file formats and sizes

Natural results and accurate presentation

Our aim is to make rooms clear and inviting without creating an unrealistic impression. We do not knowingly remove permanent features or conceal property issues in a way that could mislead a viewer.

How the process works

Share your source files, reference style, output requirements, project volume and preferred deadline. We review the brief, confirm scope and pricing, complete the agreed work, carry out a quality check and deliver the files in the agreed format.

Who this service supports

FrameCrest works with real estate photographers, photography and media studios, agents, brokerages, property managers and property marketing teams. One-off projects, recurring batches and approved white-label arrangements can be discussed.

Frequently asked questions

Can you edit batches of property photos?

Yes. The brief can cover a single property or recurring image batches. Scope and turnaround are confirmed before work begins.

Can you follow my editing style?

Yes. Share reference images and instructions so the team can understand the preferred colour, exposure and finishing direction.

Do you provide HDR editing?

HDR blending can be included when suitable bracketed source exposures are supplied.
