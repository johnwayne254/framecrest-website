THE FRAMECREST APPROACH

WHO WE ARE

A creative partner
for the property market.

FrameCrest Studios is a real estate visual production studio supporting photographers, media studios, estate agents, brokerages, property managers and property teams.

We bring together four connected services: real estate photo editing, virtual staging, floor plan design and real estate video editing. This gives clients one place to request the visual support they need to market a property clearly and professionally.

Our aim is straightforward: understand the brief, respect the character of the space, pay attention to the details and deliver files that are ready for their intended use.

OUR QUALITY PROMISE

Polished visuals.
Honest presentation.

Good property imagery should improve presentation without changing the facts. We aim for a natural, balanced finish and follow the agreed brief for every project.

Our editing workflow uses Lightroom and Photoshop. Each delivery is checked against the client's instructions and the intended output before it is sent.

01

Natural, consistent editing

Pay attention to exposure, colour, straight lines and consistency across the image set.

02

Accurate property representation

Do not conceal permanent issues or add features in a way that could mislead a viewer.

03

Careful file handling

Handle client files with care and follow the agreed project and delivery arrangements.

04

Responsible use of tools

Use AI-assisted tools only where appropriate, with client permission and suitable privacy and data terms.
