REAL ESTATE PHOTO EDITING · BEFORE / AFTER
01 CORE SERVICE

Real Estate
Photo Editing

Clean, balanced property photographs with a natural, consistent look across the full image set.

We refine supplied property photographs to help interiors and exteriors look clear, inviting and true to the space. The aim is a polished result that respects the property's real features rather than making it look like somewhere else.

What this can include

  • Exposure and brightness correction
  • Colour and white-balance correction
  • HDR blending where required
  • Window-view balancing and refinement
  • Straightening of verticals and horizontals
  • Perspective correction
  • Consistent colour and finish across the complete image set
Useful forProperty photographers · Media studios · Agents · Brokerages · Property managers

VIRTUAL STAGING · EMPTY / STAGED
02 CORE SERVICE

Virtual
Staging

Help viewers imagine how an empty room could be used with digital furniture, décor and considered styling.

We digitally furnish and style supplied photographs of empty rooms. The staging direction is guided by your preferred look, the purpose of the room and the audience you want to reach.

What this can include

  • Digital furnishing of empty rooms
  • Furniture and décor selection to suit the intended room use
  • Styling guided by your preferred look or visual references
  • Presentation tailored to the property's target audience
  • Room-by-room staging for the images included in the brief
Useful forEmpty properties · Rental listings · Property sales marketing · Agents · Property managers

A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before we
get started.

What should I include in a project brief?

Share the service you need, the files or source materials, any reference style, the expected output format, project volume and deadline. We will confirm the scope, price and turnaround before starting.

Can FrameCrest support ongoing work?

Yes. The company profile includes both single projects and recurring production support. Arrangements are agreed around the service and expected workload.

Can you work under my agency or studio brand?

White-label delivery is available for approved agency partners. The branding and delivery arrangement should be discussed and agreed as part of the brief.

Can you create a floor plan if I do not have measurements?

We need client-supplied measurements, a sketch or an approved source plan. We do not guess or invent dimensions. We will ask for clarification if the source information is incomplete.

Do you provide virtual staging for empty rooms?

Yes. Virtual staging digitally furnishes and styles supplied room photographs, guided by the preferred look, room purpose and intended audience. Staged images should be disclosed where required.

How do you quote a project?

Pricing is based on the service, volume, complexity and required turnaround. We confirm the scope and price before production begins. A small paid trial project is also an option.
