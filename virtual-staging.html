Digital furniture and décor for empty rooms

Virtual staging adds digital furniture, décor and styling to photographs of vacant or sparsely furnished rooms. It can help buyers and renters understand the possible use of a space before visiting in person.

What virtual staging can include

  • Digital furnishing of empty living rooms, bedrooms, dining areas and other spaces
  • Furniture and décor selected for the room's purpose
  • Styling guided by your references or preferred design direction
  • Consistent presentation across multiple rooms in one property
  • Perspective, scale, lighting and shadow checks

Transparent property marketing

Virtual staging is a digital visualisation, not a physical renovation or furnishing service. Staged images should be clearly identified or disclosed wherever required by the applicable listing platform, professional rules or local law. Staging should never be used to hide defects or change permanent property features.

How the process works

Share your source files, reference style, output requirements, project volume and preferred deadline. We review the brief, confirm scope and pricing, complete the agreed work, carry out a quality check and deliver the files in the agreed format.

Who this service supports

FrameCrest works with real estate photographers, photography and media studios, agents, brokerages, property managers and property marketing teams. One-off projects, recurring batches and approved white-label arrangements can be discussed.

Frequently asked questions

Can you stage several rooms in one property?

Yes. Include the rooms and desired style in the project brief so the scope can be confirmed.

Can I request a particular interior design style?

Yes. Provide visual references or describe the look you want the team to follow.

Will staged images be labelled?

We recommend clear identification as virtually staged and follow the disclosure requirements specified for the project and its intended listing platform.
